# FFF Budget Check 1.0.0

Copy the whole **Budget Check** worksheet into a compatible budget, then double-click its coloured row 3. The linked report checks H/I formulas, missing inputs, copied references, subtotal gaps, summary reconciliation, section markers and fringe inputs. Budget values and formulas are left unchanged.

Fringe findings include the B-column role title and acknowledge legitimate fees with zero fringes. Subtotal reviews show the actual I formula and omitted H rows, including zero-value lines.

## Downloads

- **FFF_Budget_Check.xls**: independent one-sheet checker for desktop Excel.
- **FFF_Budget_Check_v1.0.0.zip**: workbook, GPL-3.0 licence, guide and complete corresponding source.
- **SHA256SUMS**: checksum of the standalone workbook.

Save your recipient budget as .xls or .xlsm to retain VBA. Copy the whole worksheet, not only its cells. Enable macros for the workbook you use; no global security changes are needed.

## Compatibility and limits

Tested in desktop Excel for Mac. The supplied Finnish layout and fictional release fixtures were tested. English labels are recognised, but a complete English-template run and Windows compatibility remain unverified. Requires recognisable A section markers, D/F/G inputs and H/I totals; K/L helpers are optional. Custom formulas and ambiguous layouts request manual review. Findings are a dated snapshot and do not automatically repair the budget.

Templates and production budgets are not included. Budget comparison is outside this release.
